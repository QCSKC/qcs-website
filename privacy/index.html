Privacy Policy

How we handle information submitted through this website.

Last updated: July 2026

Overview

Quality Cable Systems ("QCS," "we," or "us") operates qualitycablesystems.com. This policy explains what information we collect when you visit our site, how we use it, and who we share it with. We keep this simple because our site is simple: it is a marketing and information site. We do not sell products online, we do not host customer accounts, and we do not run a storefront.

By using this website, you agree to the practices described below.

Information we collect

We collect only what we need to respond to inquiries and to understand how our site is used.

Information you submit

When you complete a contact or quote form, we collect the details you provide, which may include your name, company, email address, phone number, and any message or project information you choose to share.

Standard web analytics

Like most websites, we use analytics tools (such as Google Analytics) that automatically collect standard technical data: pages visited, approximate location, browser and device type, and referring source. This data is aggregated and is not used to personally identify you.

Cookies

Our analytics tools set cookies to measure site traffic and usage patterns. You can disable cookies in your browser settings. The site remains functional without them.

What we do not collect

We do not collect payment information, and we do not operate user accounts or logins on this site. We do not knowingly collect information from children.

How we use your information

We use the information you submit and the analytics we collect to:

  • Respond to your quote requests, questions, and other inquiries.
  • Communicate with you about your project or a potential working relationship.
  • Understand how visitors use our site so we can improve its content and performance.
  • Maintain the security and proper operation of the website.

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information. We do not send marketing email to people who have only browsed the site.

Who we share information with

We share information only in these limited circumstances:

  • Service providers. We use third-party tools to operate our site, such as our website host and analytics provider. These providers process data on our behalf and only for the purposes described here.
  • Legal requirements. We may disclose information if required by law, regulation, legal process, or a valid governmental request.
  • Business protection. We may disclose information where necessary to protect the rights, property, or safety of QCS, our customers, or others.

Data retention and security

We keep contact and inquiry information for as long as needed to respond to your request and to maintain a record of our business communications, then dispose of it in the ordinary course. We take reasonable measures to protect the information we hold, but no method of transmission or storage is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

Your choices

You can request that we correct or delete the contact information you have submitted, or ask what information we hold about you, by emailing us at the address below. You can also control cookies through your browser and opt out of Google Analytics using the tools Google provides.

Third-party links

Our site may link to external sites, such as our LinkedIn page. We are not responsible for the privacy practices of sites we do not operate. We encourage you to review the privacy policy of any site you visit.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. When we do, we will revise the "Last updated" date at the top of this page. Continued use of the site after a change means you accept the updated policy.

Questions about your privacy?

Contact us and we will respond. Email info@qualitycablesystems.com or write to Quality Cable Systems, 3711 Gardner, Kansas City, MO 64120.
